User Guide

Everything you need to know about I am - Aligned.

Getting Started

When you first open I am - Aligned, you'll be guided through a short onboarding where you select your goals — the areas of life you're focused on manifesting (career, relationships, health, abundance, etc.). These goals shape the AI-generated content you receive in every session.

The Mindset Check-In

Every check-in follows a four-step ritual:

  1. Vibe Check — Tap the emoji that matches how you're feeling right now. This is based on the Abraham-Hicks emotional guidance scale, from low vibration to high.
  2. Name the Thought — Optionally describe the thought or trigger affecting your mood. This helps the AI personalize your session. You can skip this step.
  3. Affirmation — Receive a personalized affirmation crafted by AI based on your goals, mood, and context. If spoken word is enabled, it's read aloud.
  4. Gratitude & Reflection — Continue through a gratitude prompt and closing reflection, each personalized to your current state.

App Lock

The core feature. Choose which apps trigger a mindset check-in when you open them. Before the app launches, I am - Aligned intercepts with a quick ritual. After completing it, you choose to proceed to the app or go home.

Setting Up App Lock

  1. Open Settings and tap "Enable Accessibility Service"
  2. Grant the accessibility permission in Android Settings
  3. Tap "Choose Apps to Lock" and select the apps you want to intercept

Tip: Start with 2-3 apps you find most distracting (social media, news, etc.). You can always add more later. After completing a check-in, the app won't intercept again for 30 minutes — so normal app switching isn't disrupted.

Scheduled Check-Ins

Beyond app-lock triggers, you can schedule regular check-ins:

  • Morning ritual — Start your day with intention. Configure the time in Settings.
  • Evening ritual — Reflect on your day. Configure the time in Settings.
  • Throughout the day — Set an interval (every 2 hours, etc.) for periodic check-ins during waking hours. Quiet hours prevent late-night interruptions.

Manual Check-In

Tap "Check In" on the main screen anytime you want to do a session. You can also use the accessibility shortcut button for instant access from anywhere.

Spoken Affirmations

Enable spoken word in Settings to have your affirmations, gratitude prompts, and reflections read aloud by an AI voice. Choose from six voices:

  • Serena — Whisper-like, meditative (default)
  • Aria — Warm, natural
  • Luna — Soft, British
  • Sage — Clear, grounded
  • James — Steady, calm
  • Oliver — Warm, British

You can also enable "Listen Only" mode, which hides the text and lets you close your eyes while the affirmations are spoken.

Ambient Music

Meditative singing bowl music plays during your ritual. Choose from five tracks in Settings, or disable ambient sound entirely.

Privacy

I am - Aligned has no user accounts. All your data — goals, moods, preferences — stays on your device. AI requests contain no personally identifiable information. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
