User Guide

Everything you need to know about I am - Aligned.

Getting Started

When you first open I am - Aligned, a short onboarding asks about you: how you're feeling lately, what you're manifesting (career, relationships, health, abundance, and more), how you want to feel, and what tends to get in your way. Every question is optional. Your answers shape the content of every session, and your first affirmation is written from them right at the end.

Sessions start with a calm voice and meditative music turned on. You can change the voice or the music, or turn either off, anytime in Settings.

The Mindset Check-In

Every check-in follows a short ritual:

  1. Vibe Check — Tap the emoji that matches how you're feeling right now. This is based on the Abraham-Hicks emotional guidance scale, from low vibration to high.
  2. Name the Feeling — Optionally pick the word that fits best, or skip it.
  3. What's on your mind? — Write the thought behind the feeling, in a few words or a few sentences. This is what makes your session personal, so it's worth a moment. If you'd rather not write, tap "or choose a topic to focus on" and pick one instead.
  4. Affirmation — A statement written for this exact moment, addressing your thought or topic.
  5. Practice — A short guided practice to shift how you feel: gratitude, a memory of feeling fulfilled, a visualization, a few slow breaths, and more. It changes from session to session.
  6. Reflection — A closing thought that ties back to what you brought in.

Move through each screen at your own pace: tap anywhere when you're ready for the next one. With voice on, you can close your eyes and listen, and the screen stays on until you're done.

Tip: Somewhere you can't play sound? Tap the speaker in the top corner of a check-in to mute the voice and music for just that session.

App Lock

The core feature. Choose which apps trigger a mindset check-in when you open them. Before the app launches, I am - Aligned intercepts with a quick ritual. After completing it, you choose to proceed to the app or go home.

Setting Up App Lock

  1. Open Settings and tap "Enable Accessibility Service"
  2. Grant the accessibility permission in Android Settings
  3. Tap "Choose Apps to Lock" and select the apps you want to intercept

Tip: Start with 2-3 apps you find most distracting (social media, news, etc.). You can always add more later. After completing a check-in, the app won't intercept again for 30 minutes — so normal app switching isn't disrupted.

Scheduled Check-Ins

Beyond app-lock triggers, you can schedule regular check-ins:

  • Morning ritual — Start your day with intention, at any time from 4 am to noon.
  • Evening ritual — Reflect on your day, at any time from 4 pm to midnight.
  • Throughout the day — Set an interval (every 2 hours, etc.) for periodic check-ins during waking hours. Quiet hours prevent late-night interruptions.

Every reminder carries an affirmation, often one of your own from a past session, so it can lift you even when you don't have time to check in. Reminders are spaced out so you never get two back to back.

Manual Check-In

Tap "Check in now" on the main screen anytime you want a session, or tap one of the topics under "Or focus on…" to start a session centered on that area of your life. Once app lock is on, you can also use the accessibility shortcut button for instant access from anywhere.

Spoken Affirmations

Your affirmation, practice, and reflection are read aloud by a calm AI voice. Choose from six voices in Settings, or turn spoken word off:

  • Serena — Whisper-like, meditative (default)
  • Aria — Warm, natural
  • Luna — Soft, British
  • Sage — Clear, grounded
  • James — Steady, calm
  • Oliver — Warm, British

You can also enable "Listen Only" mode, which hides the text and lets you close your eyes while the affirmations are spoken.

Ambient Music

Meditative singing bowl music plays through your check-in. Choose from five tracks in Settings, or turn ambient sound off.

Light Mode

I am - Aligned is dark by default, for calm check-ins at any hour. Prefer a lighter look? Turn on Light mode in Settings → Appearance for soft cream and lavender.

Offline

No connection? A check-in still works: the app replays one of your recent sessions, with its voice if you heard it before, and the music is built in.

Privacy

I am - Aligned has no user accounts. Your profile, moods, thoughts, and past sessions are stored on your device. To write a session, the details it needs are sent to our AI service and not kept. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
