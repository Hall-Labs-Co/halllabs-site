Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

I am - Aligned is designed with privacy at its core. Your profile, mood history, and goals are stored on your device. There are no user accounts. To create your personalized sessions, the details you choose to share are sent to our server and our AI provider, used to generate that session, and not stored on our servers. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

See also: Terms of Service — covers mental health disclaimers, AI content limitations, accessibility service usage, and subscription terms.

This privacy policy describes how Hall Labs LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the I am - Aligned app for Android (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information You Provide

The App allows you to:

  • Answer optional setup questions that make up your profile: your first name, age range, what you're manifesting and how you describe it, how you want to feel, what gets in your way, who you're becoming, your values, your life situation, relationship status, spiritual preferences, the first names and roles of people who matter to you, what lifts your mood, and when you tend to scroll
  • Select personal goals and intentions (e.g., career growth, self-love, abundance)
  • Record your current emotional state through mood check-ins
  • Identify thoughts or triggers affecting your mood, or choose a topic to focus a session on
  • Choose which apps trigger a mindset check-in
  • Configure preferences for voice, ambient sound, and check-in schedules

Every profile question is optional and can be skipped. This information is stored locally on your device. The parts needed to personalize a session are sent with each content request, as described below. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for analytics.

How Data Is Stored

All personal data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your profile, goals, mood history, and preferences are saved in local device storage (Android DataStore).
  • Session logs (timestamps, mood before/after, session type, and the thought you wrote) are saved in local device storage.
  • The content of your 20 most recent sessions is saved on your device, so a check-in still works offline and reminders can show your own past affirmations. Audio clips you've listened to are also kept on your device (up to about 10 MB) so offline sessions keep their voice. Older sessions and clips are removed automatically.
  • Topics you've recently focused on are saved to show them first next time.
  • No personal data is stored on our servers.
  • There are no user accounts — the App operates entirely anonymously.

Data Sent to Our Servers

When the App generates personalized content, it sends a request to our AI backend containing:

  • Your current mood level (a number from 1-5) and emotion, if selected
  • Your selected goal categories
  • Time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, night)
  • Streak count (number of consecutive days)
  • The category of the intercepted app, if applicable (e.g., "social_media" — not the specific app name)
  • Your thought/trigger text, if you chose to enter one, and the topic you chose to focus on, if any
  • Your profile answers, if you gave them (see "Information You Provide"), including any first names you entered

Requests do not include an email address, account, advertising ID, or device identifier, and are not linked to an identity. Each request carries a random install identifier, created when you first open the App and reset if you reinstall, which is used only to prevent abuse through rate limiting and is kept in server memory for no more than a day. Our backend passes the request to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your content and does not store the request contents. Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived connection logs.

Text-to-Speech

If you enable spoken affirmations, the text of your personalized content is sent to a third-party text-to-speech service (Replicate) to generate audio. This text contains only the affirmation/reflection content, which may occasionally include your first name if you shared one. Audio is generated on demand and is not stored on our servers. The App keeps the audio you've heard on your device, as described in "How Data Is Stored".

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled and created on your device. A reminder may show an affirmation, sometimes one from your own past sessions. Depending on your Android notification settings, reminders can appear on your lock screen, where others may see them.

Accessibility Service

The App uses Android's AccessibilityService API to detect when you open apps you've chosen to lock. The accessibility service:

  • Only monitors which app is currently in the foreground — it does not read screen content, keystrokes, notifications, or any other data.
  • Only activates for apps you have explicitly selected in the App's settings.
  • Runs entirely on-device — no information about your app usage is sent to our servers or any third party.
  • Can be disabled at any time in Android Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic — generates your personalized content from the request described above. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records basic app usage, such as which setup step you reached, using an app instance identifier. Your answers, mood entries, and thoughts are never sent to analytics. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the App crashes, sends a crash report so we can fix the problem: the technical details of the error, your device model, Android version, App version, and an installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your profile, answers, mood entries, or thoughts. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — used to deliver scheduled check-in reminders. Firebase may process a device token for push notification delivery. No personal data is sent through push notifications. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Remote Config — used to manage feature flags. No personal data is sent. Firebase privacy information.
  • RevenueCat — used to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase data via Google Play. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Replicate — used for text-to-speech generation. Only affirmation text is sent. Replicate privacy policy.

The App does not include any advertising SDKs.

Data Retention and Deletion

You are in full control of your data:

  • Your profile, goals, and preferences can be edited at any time in Settings → Your profile.
  • All app data is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Since no personal data is stored on our servers, there is no remote data to delete.

Sensitive Data

We recognize that mood data, emotional states, relationship status, spiritual or religious preferences, and personal goals may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act). Sharing any of it is optional. It is stored on your device, sent only to generate the session you requested, used for no other purpose, and not stored on our servers or linked to an account. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it to infer characteristics about you beyond personalizing your content.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
