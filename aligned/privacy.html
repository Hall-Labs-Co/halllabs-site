Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

I am - Aligned is designed with privacy at its core. Your profile, mood history, and goals are stored on your device. There are no user accounts. To create your personalized sessions, the details you choose to share are sent to our server and our AI provider, used to generate that session, and not stored on our servers. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

See also: Terms of Service — covers mental health disclaimers, AI content limitations, accessibility service usage, and subscription terms.

This privacy policy describes how Hall Labs LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the I am - Aligned app for Android (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information You Provide

The App allows you to:

  • Answer optional setup questions that make up your profile: such as your first name and age range, your goals and how you want to feel, your values and life situation, your relationship status, your spiritual or religious preferences, the first names of people who matter to you, and your habits
  • Select personal goals and intentions (e.g., career growth, self-love, abundance)
  • Record your current emotional state through mood check-ins
  • Identify thoughts or triggers affecting your mood, or choose a topic to focus a session on
  • Choose which apps trigger a mindset check-in
  • Configure preferences for voice, ambient sound, and check-in schedules

Every profile question is optional and can be skipped. This information is stored locally on your device. The parts needed to personalize a session are sent with each content request, as described below. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for analytics.

How Data Is Stored

All personal data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your profile, goals, mood history, and preferences.
  • A history of your check-ins, including your mood and any thought you wrote.
  • Your recent sessions and their audio, so the App works offline and reminders can show your own past affirmations. Older ones are removed automatically.
  • No personal data is stored on our servers.
  • There are no user accounts — the App operates entirely anonymously.

Data Sent to Our Servers

When the App generates personalized content, it sends a request to our AI backend containing:

  • How you're feeling right now
  • Your goals and profile answers, if you gave them, including any first names you entered
  • Any thought you wrote or topic you chose for this session
  • The general category of the app you were opening, if the check-in came from App Lock (never the app's name)
  • Basic context such as the time of day, your streak, your voice preference, and some of your recent session content

Requests do not include an email address, account, advertising ID, or device identifier, and are not linked to an identity. Each request carries the App's version number and a random install identifier, created when you first open the App and reset if you reinstall. The identifier is used only to prevent abuse through rate limiting and to count how many installs use each version of the App (so we know when an old version can be retired), and is kept in server memory for no more than a day. Our backend passes the request to our AI provider to generate your content and does not store the request contents. Our AI provider does not use this data to train its models, and may keep requests for a limited time under its own policy, for example to detect misuse. Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived connection logs. Our backend also keeps anonymous service statistics for one day, such as how long sessions and audio take to create and whether they failed, with no request content or identifiers.

Text-to-Speech

If you enable spoken affirmations, the text of your personalized content is sent to a text-to-speech provider to generate audio (if our main provider is slow, the same text may also be sent to a backup provider). This text contains only the affirmation/reflection content, which may occasionally include your first name if you shared one. Audio is generated on demand and is not stored on our servers; the provider may keep it briefly under its own policy. The App keeps the audio you've heard on your device, as described in "How Data Is Stored".

Emailing Us

If you email us, for example with "Share your thoughts" or the support link in Settings, we receive your email address and whatever you write, in our email inbox. We use them only to read and reply to your message, keep them only as long as needed for that, and never add you to a mailing list. To help us answer, the App fills in a few details at the end of the draft, where you can see them and delete them before sending: the App's version, your Android version, and your app ID (the random identifier our subscription provider uses, which we use only to look up your subscription). Nothing about your profile, moods or sessions is included.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled and created on your device. A reminder may show an affirmation, sometimes one from your own past sessions. Depending on your Android notification settings, reminders can appear on your lock screen, where others may see them.

Crisis Support

If something you write in a check-in suggests you may be in crisis, the App may show crisis line information, such as 988 in the US. Your words are checked on your device and by the AI while it writes your session. Nothing about this is stored, reported to anyone, or sent to analytics, and no person reads what you write. This check is automated and can miss signs; it is not a crisis service.

Accessibility Service

The App uses Android's AccessibilityService API to detect when you open apps you've chosen to lock. The accessibility service:

  • Only monitors which app is currently in the foreground — it does not read screen content, keystrokes, notifications, or any other data.
  • Only activates for apps you have explicitly selected in the App's settings.
  • Runs on-device. When it opens a check-in, only a general category of the app (for example social media, finance, dating, shopping, entertainment or news) is sent with that check-in so the session can speak to the moment. The app's name, your list of locked apps and when you open them are never sent, and the category is not stored on our servers.
  • Can be disabled at any time in Android Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • AI provider — writes your personalized content from the request described above. It does not use this data to train its models.
  • Text-to-speech providers — turn your session text into speech, if you use spoken affirmations: a main provider, and a backup used only when the main one is slow. Only the session text is sent.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records basic app usage using an app instance identifier: for example, which setup step you reached, where a check-in started, whether it was completed, the voice, music, and theme you use, the mood level (1 to 5) you picked for a check-in or a follow-up, whether you allowed notifications, when App Lock is turned on or off, which paywall you saw and which plan you chose, and what a session cost us to create. Analytics never receives your profile answers, the words you choose to describe a feeling, the topics you pick, or anything you write. You can turn analytics off anytime in Settings → Privacy; in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland it stays off unless you turn it on. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the App crashes, sends a crash report so we can fix the problem: the technical details of the error, your device model, Android version, App version, and an installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your profile, answers, mood entries, or thoughts. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — lets us send an occasional message or settings update to the App. Your reminders don't use it; they're created on your device. Firebase processes a device token to deliver messages. No personal data is sent through push notifications. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Remote Config — used to manage feature flags. It uses an installation identifier only; no personal data is sent. Firebase privacy information.
  • Subscription provider — manages subscriptions, processing anonymous purchase data from Google Play. The App shares its analytics instance identifier with it, and it reports subscription events (such as a trial starting, a renewal, or a cancellation) to Google Analytics, so we can see how many people subscribe. Your name, email, and payment details are never shared with us.

For a current list of the service providers we use, email info@halllabs.co.

The App does not include any advertising SDKs.

Data Retention and Deletion

You are in full control of your data:

  • Your profile, goals, and preferences can be edited at any time in Settings → Your profile.
  • All app data is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • We don't store your personal data, check-ins or thoughts on our servers, so there is nothing of that kind to delete remotely.
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports expire on their own (analytics within 14 months, crash reports within about 90 days). You can turn analytics off anytime in Settings → Privacy.
  • To request deletion of any data we hold, email info@halllabs.co with the subject "Delete my data". We'll respond within 30 days.

Sensitive Data

We recognize that mood data, emotional states, relationship status, spiritual or religious preferences, and personal goals may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act). Sharing any of it is optional. It is stored on your device, sent only to generate the session you requested, and not stored on our servers or linked to an account. The one exception is the 1 to 5 mood level of each check-in, which is also included in anonymous usage analytics (see "Third-Party Services") so we can understand how the App helps; it is not linked to your identity. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it to infer characteristics about you beyond personalizing your content.

Users in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you live in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the following also applies.

Who is responsible. Hall Labs LLC, California, United States, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact us at info@halllabs.co.

Why we use your data, and on what legal basis:

  • Creating your sessions (your mood, the thoughts you write, and your profile answers, which may reveal information about your health or beliefs): your explicit consent, which you give on the "Your privacy" screen when you first open the App (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Because sessions are written from this information, you withdraw consent by no longer using the App and uninstalling it, which deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Usage analytics: your consent. In these regions analytics is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings → Privacy.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the App working (Article 6(1)(f)). Crash reports contain only technical details, never your profile, moods, or thoughts.
  • Preventing abuse and keeping the App working (the random install identifier, used for rate limiting and for counting installs on each App version): our legitimate interest in protecting the service and knowing when an old version can be retired.
  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in replying to you.
  • Subscriptions: to perform our contract with you, through Google Play and our subscription provider.

International transfers. Our servers and service providers are located in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK and Swiss equivalents).

How long data is kept. The content of your requests is not stored on our servers. The install identifier is kept in server memory for no more than a day. Usage analytics is kept for no more than 14 months, and crash reports for about 90 days. Everything on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Most of your data exists only on your device, where you can view and edit it in the App and delete it by uninstalling. For analytics or crash data, or any other request, email info@halllabs.co. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
