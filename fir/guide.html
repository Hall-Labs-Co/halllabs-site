Fir User Guide

Scan food products, get personalized health grades based on your allergies and dietary preferences, and see who really makes your food.

Getting Started

1. Set Up Your Profile

When you first open Fir, you'll set up your personal sensitivity profile. Choose from 25+ templates covering medical allergies, dietary conditions, and lifestyle preferences. You can adjust severity levels and add custom sensitivities.

2. Start Your Free Trial

After setting up your profile, you'll be prompted to start a 7-day free trial. You get full access to all features during the trial with no charge. Cancel anytime before the trial ends.

3. Scan a Product

Point your camera at a product's barcode. Fir will instantly look up the product and give you a personalized grade. Keep the camera pointed at the ingredient label for enhanced AI analysis.

Understanding Your Results

Letter Grades

Every product gets a letter grade from A+ to F based on three factors:

  • Nutrition Score — based on Nutri-Score data (calories, fat, sugar, fiber, protein)
  • Processing Score — based on NOVA food processing classification and additive analysis
  • Personal Match — based on how well the product matches your specific sensitivity profile

The weight of each factor depends on your profile type. Medical profiles weight personal match heavily; diet profiles balance all three more evenly.

Flagged Ingredients

Ingredients that match your sensitivities are highlighted in color-coded chips:

  • Red — matches a sensitivity you've marked as severe/allergic
  • Orange — matches a moderate sensitivity (intolerant)
  • Yellow — matches a mild preference (prefer to avoid)

Brand Trust

For products with AI analysis, you'll see a brand trust card showing parent company ownership, trust grade, and any concerns about ingredient changes after acquisitions.

Data Sources

Chips at the bottom of each result show where the data came from:

  • Barcode — product data from the Open Food Facts database
  • Label — ingredient text read from the physical label via OCR
  • AI — enhanced analysis from our AI service

Tip: After scanning a barcode, keep pointing your camera at the ingredient label. Fir will read the label and send it to AI for deeper analysis — especially useful for trace allergen warnings that aren't in the barcode database.

Important Limitations

Fir is an informational tool, not a safety device. Please read our Terms of Service for full details. Key points:

  • Always read the physical product label — it is the most authoritative source of ingredient information.
  • Product databases may be outdated or incorrect. Formulations change without notice.
  • The App cannot detect manufacturing cross-contamination or shared-facility risks.
  • A positive grade does not mean a product is safe for you to consume.
  • Fir is not a substitute for professional medical advice from a doctor or allergist.

Managing Your Subscription

Your subscription is managed through Google Play. To change or cancel:

  • Open Google Play Store on your phone
  • Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • Find Fir and tap Manage

If you switch devices, tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen to recover your subscription.
