Riser User Guide

Paragliding variometer for Wear OS — complete guide to all features

Auto-Detect Flights

Tap the complication, go fly. Riser detects takeoff and landing automatically — no buttons to press while airborne.

Live Tracking

Connect your XCTracer, Skydrop, or BlueFly via Bluetooth. Superior vario data on your wrist with wind and dual battery display.

Getting Started

Riser turns your Wear OS watch into a paragliding variometer with audio and haptic feedback. Install from the Google Play Store on both your watch and phone.

First Launch

  1. Open Riser on your watch
  2. Grant location permissions when prompted
  3. Go to Settings and set your pilot name, glider type, and preferred units
  4. Set Feedback Mode to "Audio + Haptic" for full vario feedback
Tip: Make sure your watch's ring and notification volume is turned up. Riser uses the alarm audio channel to ensure vario beeps are audible even in wind.

Flying with Riser

Manual Flight Start

Opening Riser lands you on the home screen, which immediately connects to GPS and displays connection status. When you're ready to fly, tap the play button in the center of the screen. Riser will:

  • Calibrate the barometer against GPS altitude
  • Begin audio/haptic vario feedback
  • Record your flight track as an IGC file

In-Flight Screens

Swipe up/down to switch between two screens:

  • Thermal Screen — Altitude, vertical speed (vario), ground speed, glide ratio. Your primary screen while thermalling.
  • Glide/Nav Screen — Distance, max altitude, elapsed time, battery level. Check during glides between thermals.

In-Flight Controls

  • Long-press — Lock the screen to prevent accidental scrolls. Long-press again to unlock.
  • Double-tap — Pause or resume the flight.
  • Swipe left to right — Open the controls screen with Pause, Lock, and Stop buttons.

To stop a flight, swipe to the controls screen and tap Stop. Your flight is automatically saved and synced to the companion phone app.

Auto-Detect with the Complication

Riser can automatically detect when you're flying and start tracking — no need to open the app. This is controlled by a watch face complication.

Setting Up the Complication

  1. Long-press your watch face → tap Customize
  2. Tap any complication slot
  3. Scroll to find Riser — Flight Detect
  4. Select it — you'll see a paraglider icon with a circular ring

Arming & Disarming

  • Armed (filled ring) — detection is active for 2 hours with full GPS

  • Two quick vibrations confirm arming, one short vibration confirms disarming
Pre-flight routine: Tap the complication before heading to launch. Detection monitors barometer + GPS for flight signatures. When it detects you're flying, your watch vibrates and a notification pops up — tap once to start tracking.

How Detection Works

Riser uses multiple signals to detect flight:

  • Thermal detection — Sustained altitude gain with high climb rate variance (circling in thermals)
  • Ridge detection — Sustained speed with frequent bearing changes (ridge soaring back and forth)
  • Elevation confirmation — Google Elevation API confirms you're above ground level, filtering out driving on hills

Auto-Detect Landing

When the complication is armed and a flight is being tracked, Riser also monitors for landing. When your speed drops below walking pace for 90 seconds, you'll be prompted to stop the flight — either via an in-app prompt or a notification if the app is in the background.

Note: The complication monitors for 2 hours after arming. If no flight is detected in that time, it automatically disarms (ring empties). Re-tap to arm again for another 2-hour window.

Live Tracking

Share your position in real-time so friends and retrieve crew can watch your flight live.

LiveTrack24

  2. In Riser Settings → Live Tracking → enable LiveTrack24
  3. Enter your username and password
  4. During flight, your position is sent every 5 seconds

SportsTrackLive

Riser also supports SportsTrackLive for 3D live tracking. Enable it in Settings → Live Tracking → SportsTrackLive, and enter your email and password. Your flights will be uploaded automatically and viewable in their 3D viewer.

Companion Phone App

The Riser companion app on your Android phone receives flight logs automatically from your watch. For each flight you can:

  • Share — Export the IGC file to any app
  • Upload to XContest — Submit directly to the world's largest paragliding competition platform
  • Upload to SportsTrackLive — View your flight in 3D
  • Name your flights — Tap the edit icon to add a memorable name
  • Delete — Remove unwanted flights

Flights sync automatically when your watch and phone are connected. Tap the refresh button to request missing flights.

External BLE Vario

Connect a dedicated Bluetooth vario (XCTracer, Skydrop, BlueFlyVario, Stodeus) for superior pressure data. The hardware vario's sensor replaces the watch's built-in barometer.

Pairing a Device

  1. In Settings → External Vario → enable BLE Vario
  2. Tap Device to scan for nearby BLE varios
  3. Select your device from the list
  4. Riser auto-connects at the start of each flight

When connected, the Glide/Nav screen shows wind data (if your vario sends LXWP0) and both watch + vario battery levels. The Thermal screen shows an "EXT VARIO" indicator at the bottom.

Automatic Fallback

If the BLE vario disconnects mid-flight (out of range, battery dies), Riser seamlessly falls back to the watch's built-in barometer. No data gap, no user action needed. When the vario reconnects, Riser switches back automatically.

Settings Reference

Pilot & Glider

  • Pilot Name — Embedded in IGC files
  • Glider Type — Your wing model (e.g., "Advance Alpha 7")
  • Registration — Glider registration number

Feedback

  • Feedback Mode — Audio + Haptic, Audio Only, Haptic Only, or Off
  • Feedback Profile — Classic, Sensitive, Mellow — adjusts audio tone mapping and haptic intensity

Units

  • Use Imperial — Toggle sets all units at once (ft, mph, mi, ft/min)
  • Individual units can be customized: vertical speed, ground speed, altitude, distance

Demo

  • Demo Flight — Simulates a realistic paragliding flight with changing altitude, speed, and vario data. Great for testing audio/haptic feedback and familiarizing yourself with the in-flight screens before your first real flight.

Flight Log

Riser keeps a log of all your recorded flights on the watch. From the home screen, tap the flight log icon to view past flights with stats including:

  • Date and time
  • Duration
  • Max altitude
  • Distance
  • Max speed, max climb rate, average climb rate

You can sync individual flights to your phone, delete flights, or view detailed stats. Flights are also synced automatically to the companion phone app when your watch and phone are connected.

Tips & Troubleshooting

No vario audio? Check that Feedback Mode is set to "Audio + Haptic" or "Audio Only" in Riser Settings. Also ensure your watch's ring volume is turned up — if your phone is on silent/vibrate and syncs that state to the watch, the watch may be muted too. Riser uses the alarm audio channel which should bypass silent mode, but some watch settings can still suppress it.
GPS not connecting? Make sure location permissions are granted. GPS may take 30-60 seconds to get a first fix, especially if the watch hasn't been used outdoors recently.
Flights not syncing to phone? Ensure your watch and phone are connected via Bluetooth. Open the phone app and tap the refresh button to request missing flights.
Complication not detecting flights? Make sure the ring is filled (armed). The complication monitors for 2 hours after tapping — re-arm if your session is longer.
